# Replix Freemium Architecture & Gating Strategy

This document outlines the strict division of features between the Free tier and Replix Pro. The core philosophy is to **give away the game (habit-building, volume, and social virality) for free, and monetize the stats (deep analytics, history, and competitor intel).**

## Core Tracking & Identity
| Feature | Free Tier | Replix Pro |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Workout Tracking** | Unlimited daily workouts | Unlimited daily workouts |
| **AI Form Detection** | 100% Free (Squats, Pushups, Planks) | 100% Free |
| **Audio Coaching** | Standard real-time feedback | Standard real-time feedback |
| **Profile Identity** | Basic Avatar upload | Basic Avatar + Pro border & tag |
| **Social Network** | Unlimited friends & followers | Unlimited friends & followers |
| **Gamification** | All Daily Quests, XP, & Achievements | All Daily Quests, XP, & Achievements |

## Leaderboards & Social Competition
| Feature | Free Tier | Replix Pro |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weekly Leaderboards** | Full access (Global & Friends) | Full access |
| **Monthly Leaderboards** | **Locked** (Hidden behind Paywall) | Full access |
| **Background Ranking** | Ranks calculated (Ghost Ranking) | Ranks calculated & visible |

## Competitor Insights (Profile Clicks)
| Feature | Free Tier | Replix Pro |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vanity Metrics** | Visible (Rank, XP, Level, Trophies) | Visible |
| **Performance Intel** | **Locked** (Blurred teaser) | Visible (Streak, Total Volume) |
| **Max PRs** | **Locked** (Blurred teaser) | Visible (Max Pushups, Squats, Planks) |

## Recap Modals & Social Sharing
| Feature | Free Tier | Replix Pro |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weekly Recap** | Full access to Podium & Share Card | Full access |
| **Weekly Grid Stats** | 100% Free (XP, Reps, Plank, AI Precision, Active Days) | Full access |
| **Social Export** | Standard share card | Standard card + Pro avatar border & tag |
| **Monthly Recap** | **Locked** (Teaser state) | Full access |
| **Progress History** | **Locked** ("Show Progress" button) | Full week-over-week access |

## Analytics & History
| Feature | Free Tier | Replix Pro |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Graphs** | Weekly graph only | Visible (Monthly/Yearly graphs) |
| **History View** | Current month history only | Full access to all past months |
